📖 How fighter stats work

The basics: when you scan an object, the scanner reports facts (weight, size, material, up to 3 properties, condition, Aura). The code turns those facts into stats with fixed rules — the dice decide every hit, so nobody can rig the result.

Ranks

S 43–50 · A 37–42 · B 31–36 · C 25–30 · D 19–24 · E 13–18 · F 5–12 total stat points. Stats are scaled evenly until the total fits the rank. Condition is applied last: New +1 Durability (Mint), Worn −1 Durability +1 Aura (Battle-worn), Damaged −2 Durability +1 Aura.

Moves & battle

Each fighter has 3 moves: Move 1 on odd rounds, Move 2 on even rounds, and a special used once (round 3) for double damage. Damage = Strength + dice (1–6), doubled/halved by the matchup (liquid>electronic/paper, sharp>paper/fabric/plastic, heavy>glass/ceramic, hot>food/plastic; sharp is halved vs flexible), ×2 for specials, ×1.5 on a critical. Battle ends on a KO or after 10 rounds — then the higher HP% wins.

🎙️ Voice attacks & rooms

During each round you can say your attack (move name, "one/two/three", or "special"). In multiplayer, create a room to get a 5-letter code and have a friend join — the server runs the fight and both of you watch the same anime episode.
